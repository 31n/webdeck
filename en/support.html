FAQ

How do I add a web app?

Tap “+” in the top right of the list and add a URL. You can also choose Webdeck from the Share button in Safari and other apps.

How do I open HTML made by AI?

Tap “+” and choose “Paste HTML” or “Import HTML File.” You can also send it to Webdeck from the Share button in the AI app.

How do I get back to the list from an open app?

Shake your iPhone, or pull down from the top of the page and hold, to minimize the app to the bar at the bottom of the screen. Tap the bar or pull it up to bring the app back. You can change what each gesture does with the gear (Settings) in the top right of the list.

Does it work offline? Will I get notifications?

Webdeck is designed to be used online. Offline use of web apps (Service Worker) and Web Push notifications are not supported. For web apps that need notifications, add them to your Home Screen from Safari.

How do I sign out or clear saved data?

Touch and hold the app, choose “Edit & Share,” then “Clear Website Data.” This removes that app's cookies and saved data.

A page looks tiny, like it's made for computers

Turn on “Fit to Phone Width” in “Edit & Share.” Pages that don't specify a screen width are then fitted to the phone's width.
