An iPhone and iPad app that brings the web apps (PWAs) you use often and HTML made by AI together in one place, and opens them in full screen without browser bars.

Collect

Add URLs and HTML files, organize them into folders, and view them as a list, grid, or cards. You can also send things to Webdeck from the Share button in other apps.

Shake to Minimize, Tap to Return

Shake your iPhone, or pull down from the top of the page and hold, to minimize the app to the bar at the bottom of the screen. Turn your iPhone upside down to switch to the previous app.

Share with a QR Code

Share your apps with a QR code or a link.

Send us your thoughts and requests with the feedback form (the form is in Japanese, but feel free to write in English).
