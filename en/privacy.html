Effective date: October 29, 2026

The developer of Webdeck (“the App”) does not collect your personal information. This page explains how the App handles data. This is a translation of the Japanese privacy policy; if there is any difference, the Japanese version prevails.

1. Information the developer collects

None. The App contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking. It does not send any data to the developer's servers.

2. Information stored on your device

  • The URLs, names, and icons of the web apps you add, the HTML you import, your folders, and your settings are stored only on your device.
  • Cookies and storage for each web app (such as sign-in status) are also stored on your device, kept separate for each web app.
  • You can remove this data by deleting the App, or with “Clear Website Data” or “Delete” in the App.

3. Communication with web apps

  • Web apps and sites you open in the App are accessed directly from your device. How those sites handle your information is governed by each site's privacy policy.
  • When you add a web app, the App fetches information from that site (its manifest and icon images) to show its name and icon.

4. Use of device features

The App uses the camera, microphone, location, or saving to Photos only when a web app you open in the App requests it, and only after you allow it in the iOS permission prompt. The information is passed only to that web app and is never sent to the developer. You can change these permissions anytime in the iOS Settings app.

5. Share sheet

HTML and URLs you send to the App from the Share button in other apps are kept temporarily on your device and added to the list when you open the App. They are not sent anywhere else.

6. In-app purchases (tips)

Tip purchases are processed by Apple's App Store. The developer does not receive your name or payment information.

7. Children

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will announce it on this page.

9. Contact

Please see the Support page.
